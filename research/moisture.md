## Gaps
- NO paper gives numeric fold-change of exudate infected vs non-infected. All qualitative.
- No continuous human moisture time series around infection.
- RH under dressing saturates (95–100%) → RH alone useless; use wetness/exudate index or impedance.
- No data on shower/detachment effect on moisture signal.
