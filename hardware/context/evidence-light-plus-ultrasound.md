Light plus ultrasound together: only lab data

This is your most novel claim and your thinnest evidence. As far as I found, one research line covers it, called CLENS. Its in-vitro work on P. acnes biofilms found that delivering both at once killed more bacteria than either alone, in both free-floating bacteria and biofilms. Other details: [nih]

- Both energy levels were within ranges the FDA had previously cleared. [mdpi]
- Skin cells showed no significant harm up to 58 J/cm² with ultrasound, measured over 24 hours. [nih]
- The proposed mechanism is that ultrasound's mechanical stress makes bacteria more vulnerable to light, but the authors call this speculative. [nih]
- Their ultrasound was about 450 kHz, much higher than the 20–40 kHz used in clinical wound devices. [ieee]
